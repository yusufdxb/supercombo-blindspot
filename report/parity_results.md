The load-bearing correctness claim for the teardown: our reimplemented openpilot v0.9.7
`supercombo` inference matches comma's own reference output, so the downstream collapse is
the model, not our reimplementation. Regenerated by `python -m src.run_parity`.

Acceptance criterion (`src/run_parity.py`): `|delta| <= 0.5 m/s^2` for `>= 95%` of frames
after a 40-frame (2 s at 20 Hz) warm-up trim. The compared quantity is the longitudinal
acceleration head `accel_t0`; reference = comma's logged modelV2 trace (rlog regen), ours =
the parsed `accel_t0` from the reimplemented path with correct recurrent-state threading and
unnormalized YUV input.
