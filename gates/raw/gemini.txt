## 2. The "Reviewer 2" Kill-Shot

**SINGLE MOST LIKELY REASON FOR REJECTION:**
**The "N=2 / Two-Fold LOCO" Statistical Thinness.**
While the paper is honest about this being an "estimate," claiming a ~1% false-positive rate based on a single leave-one-corpus-out fold across only two datasets (Subaru and RAM) is statistically fragile. A skeptic will argue the "1% FPR" is an anecdote, not a calibration.

**VERDICT: FIXABLE.**
This is an "N-problem." Adding a third or fourth real-world comma corpus (e.g., a "Toyota" or "Honda" segment) would turn the calibration from a two-fold estimate into a reportable statistic with variance.
